# Nokcandu 0.15.1

## New: controller overlay

A 3D controller that shows your buttons, sticks, triggers and trackpads as you use them, and turns with the gyro. For streaming and recording. Controls → Controller Settings → Controller overlay.

- **Over games and streams**: sits above everything, full-screen games and streams included, and never takes clicks. **Move Overlay…** lets you drag it anywhere, on any screen.
- **In its own window (for OBS)**: add it as a macOS Screen Capture source set to Window. Set Background to Colour and add a Chroma Key filter.
- **Controllers**: Steam Controller (2015), Steam Controller (2026), Xbox, Switch Pro Controller and DualShock 4 / DualSense. By default it shows the controller you're using and switches when you pick up another one.
- **What it shows**: buttons light up and press in; sticks tilt and light up while moved; a dot follows your thumb on each trackpad (and the DualShock 4 / DualSense touchpad); triggers show a partial pull with a filling bar and a full pull in solid colour; grip and back buttons light up too.
- **Settings**: size, opacity, body, button and pressed colours, background, turning with the gyro, how much of the movement to show, and Recentre. Every change applies at once.
- It only redraws when something visibly changes, at most 60 times a second, and draws nothing while the controller rests. It reads controllers without changing how they work in games. Like anything drawn over a full-screen stream, it can add a millisecond or two of latency while it's on.
