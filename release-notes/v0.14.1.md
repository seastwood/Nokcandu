# Nokcandu 0.14.1

## Streams
- **HEVC works.** Streams set to HEVC (and H.264) showed only a black picture: each frame reached the Mac's decoder in broken pieces. They now play like AV1.
- **A stream that can't be decoded ends with a message** after 5 seconds instead of staying black, and no longer floods the host with requests for new frames (which could leave Sunshine needing a restart).
- **The VBAN microphone keeps sending** when the Mac's audio changes during a stream (headphones or AirPods connecting, the default device switching). It used to go silent until VBAN was turned off and on.
- **Close works** on the message shown when a stream's connection is lost.
- Frames are copied once instead of three times and shown on the next display refresh without an extra wait, for every codec. The stream log records the video statistics every 5 seconds.

## Steam Controller trackpads
- **Slow movement no longer stalls.** The pointer used to stop for a moment at every slow-down or change of direction on a quiet trackpad, and dropped what your thumb did meanwhile.
- **Match sideways sensitivity** (when X and Y sensitivities differ): the higher one applies in full only when you move straight along its direction, so moving up and down doesn't magnify your thumb's sideways wander, while moving across keeps its full speed.
- **Straight flicks** (off by default): a quick swipe that is nearly straight up, down or across goes exactly straight.
