# Nokcandu 0.15.3

## Controller overlay: mappings on the controller

- **Show mappings** (Settings → Controller overlay): the overlay writes what each button, trackpad region and stick does in the layer that's on. Keys use their symbols (⇧ ⌃ ⌥ ⌘ ␣ ↩ ⇥ ⎋ ⌫ and the arrows), letters show as themselves, anything longer gets a short name. A hold or double tap goes small underneath, anything toggled on gets a ring, and the trackpad region your thumb is on lights up softly.
- Trackpads show their regions: four directions with their dead zone, zones (left/right, top/bottom, quadrants, cross) with their clicks, and the edge ring. A stick's directions are written around it.
- Labels too small or hidden on their buttons are written beside them: bumpers on the body's upper corners, Back and Start above their buttons, the stick click below the stick.
- Show them **always**, **while a button is held**, or **on and off with a button** (the new "Show mappings on the overlay" action).
- **Zoom in with the mappings button**: the controller grows big in the middle of the screen to read its mappings and shrinks back to its place.
- Xbox, Switch Pro, DualShock/DualSense and Steam Controller (2026) overlays put labels beside their small parts too (bumpers, View/Menu/Share, −/+/Capture/Home, Create/Options/PS, D-pad), and their back paddles show.
- The overlay shows while its settings are open or while it's being moved, even with no controller connected, so you can see what you're changing.
- A trigger's full pull shows on the trigger (⇊ and what it does), and the trigger is ringed when its full pull is toggled on.
- In a stream, labels and zooming are drawn once and only copied onto frames, so latency doesn't change.

## Streams

- **Layer, gyro and calibration notes show in full-screen streams**, drawn into the stream's picture like the overlay and statistics: no window over the stream, so no added delay. They show even with "Hold notifications while playing" on, since they no longer slow the stream down.

## Steam Controller (2015) overlay

- A truer shape, from PadSight's outline (MIT, based on VSCView by John Klein): big recessed trackpads, the centre panel, flared handles. The back grips stick out where you can see them.
- Pressing a trackpad no longer breaks up its face.
- The profile editor names its parts as Valve does: Joystick, Left/Right grip, Back ◀, Start ▶.

## Headphone motion (AirPods)

- **Its own directions (radial menu)**: head motion can work as 4 or 8 directions, each set like a button: keys, hotkeys, mouse buttons, macros, layers, menus, with tap, hold and toggle. Choose how far to turn to pick one; it holds steady near the edges.
- **Recentre slowly while in the dead zone** (optional): the centre follows your head while it stays in the dead zone, so drift fades and only deliberate turns count.
- **Recenter head motion**: a new action for any button.
- Disconnecting the headphones (or their motion stopping) lets go of whatever head motion was holding.
- **Head motion picture** on the overlay: a circle showing where your head points, the dead zone, and the directions or radial menu with what each does. Placed on its own: drag it while moving the overlay.

## Controller profiles

- **Gyro steering wheel bar**: above the overlay and live in the profile editor, showing how far you're turning, full lock and the straight range.
- **Game rumble now applies in streams**, and 0% really is off. New profiles start the Steam Controller's game rumble at 1%.
- **Trackpad zones set to "while held" hold** when you keep your thumb on them.
- **Right trackpad as a mouse-like stick** has more settings: left/right and up/down sensitivity, curve, least tilt, and how quickly it responds and returns.
- **Stick directions with scrolling**: a stick can scroll with up/down while left and right are their own buttons.
- **Trackpad key haptics**: a separate strength for moving onto a key and off it.

## App

- Tabs are now Controls, Streams, Games. Controls and Streams are marked Stable; Games is marked Experimental and suggests CodeWeavers' CrossOver for a more complete experience. A fresh install starts with Controls and Streams on.
- Streams: 100 fps and custom frame rates (whole numbers, as the streaming protocol requires).
