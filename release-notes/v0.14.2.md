# Nokcandu 0.14.2

## Steam Controller
- **Bluetooth** (new): the original Steam Controller works over Bluetooth, on firmware that supports it, with your profiles, pads, gyro and haptics as over the cable or receiver. When more than one connection is available, the cable is used first, then the receiver, then Bluetooth.
- **Trackpad acceleration**: fast swipes go further than slow ones, up to the amount you set, while slow aiming keeps your sensitivity. Off by default. **Full acceleration at** sets how fast a swipe gets all of it.
- **Gyro acceleration** is now called Acceleration (it was "Faster when turning fast"), with explanations for its settings.
- **Directional sensitivity** replaces Match sideways sensitivity, for trackpads whose X and Y sensitivities differ. **Highest toward lowest** (the default) brings the higher one down as you move toward the other's direction. You can also vary only X, only Y, both, or turn it off.
- **The wireless receiver no longer gives up on the controller** when a settings reply goes missing: the controller kept working but stopped responding until it was restarted. Controller connections, pauses and takeovers are now recorded in `~/Library/Logs/Nokcandu/Controllers.log`.

## Streams
- **After reinstalling Sunshine or Apollo**, the host shows **Pair…** instead of just "Offline". Pairing again keeps the host and its stream profiles instead of adding a second copy. A profile whose app changed number on the host finds it again by name, so Stream no longer does nothing until you pick the app again.
- **Remove a host** from its page: click its name in the sidebar.
- **Pair…** is a row in the sidebar that stays put when you hover.
- Looking for hosts on the network starts afresh each time Connect to Host opens, and what it finds is recorded in the stream log.
