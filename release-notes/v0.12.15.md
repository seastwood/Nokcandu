# Nokcandu 0.12.15

## Controller profiles

### Original Steam Controller (2015)
- **Use it with profiles, on its cable or its wireless receiver:** choose "Steam Controller" as the profile's input controller. Experimental.
- **Each trackpad can be a trackpad mouse or a trackball mouse**, with its own X and Y sensitivity, momentum after a flick, touch and movement haptics, and gyro that works only while you touch the pad.
- **Smoothing and Pad angle:** Smoothing steadies a resting or slow thumb without holding back quick movement; Pad angle lines the pad up with your thumb's sweep.
- **Only your profile's controls reach the Mac or the game.** The controller's built-in mouse, keyboard and button actions are off while Nokcandu reads it and return afterwards. Map a trigger or pad click to Left click if you want one.
- The picture shows the Steam Controller's own layout, including the rear grips.

### More in a profile
- **Import a layer** from another profile, with the macros and radial menus it uses.
- **Radial menus can be picked with the mouse or gyro**, and new choices name themselves after what they do.
- **AirPods and other head-tracking headphones** can be added to a profile as a motion source (experimental).
- **Button labels are chosen per profile** (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam).
- **Routing:** choose whether a game gets the controller's own input, your mappings, or both — for everything or control by control.
- **Controller Input Test** (Help menu) shows what your controllers are sending.
- A separate switch for the "Gyro on / Gyro off" note.

### Fixes
- **Clicking no longer moves the cursor somewhere else** in older games (Battlefield 1942 and others) when a controller is the mouse, or with a mouse while raw mouse capture is on.
- **Switch Pro and Xbox controllers are recognised correctly in Windows games** again.
- The gyro keeps its calibration when you switch windows.
- Typing a layer's name no longer loses the keyboard to another app.
- The profile editor fits narrow windows: the picture moves above its settings, explanations are behind (i) buttons, and nothing is cut off at the edges.

## Library
- **See what's running:** running games and launchers are marked green in the list and shown beside the window title on every tab.
- **Categories:** make your own sidebar sections for games, launchers or anything else (right-click → Category). Sections collapse.
- The sidebar's Profiles tab is now **Controls**. A **Streams** tab is on its way.
- The sidebar's buttons stay in place while the list scrolls, and search sits under the tabs.
