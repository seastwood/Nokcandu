# Nokcandu 0.14.4

## Smoother streaming

- **Fixed brief freezes every few seconds with a Steam Controller receiver connected but the controller off.** Empty receiver slots were being checked on the main thread, interrupting the stream. Discovery now runs in the background, with bounded scans and cancellation when the reader stops.
- A stream keeps its own macOS activity protection, whether or not a controller is attached. The existing optional AWDL setting now also applies while streaming.

## Stream statistics and graphs

- **More detailed statistics:** video payload bitrate and peak, buffered frames, host and decode min/average/max times, received/decoded/enqueued frame rates, frame assembly, decoder queue and presentation wait, audio buffering, frame drops and arrival/display/enqueue gaps.
- **Performance graphs** is a separate option from text statistics. Show either, both or neither, and change them during a stream. Graphs show the last two minutes of network RTT/variation, decode/presentation timing and frame gaps.
- The overlay uses smaller, more closely spaced text and a more transparent background. Codec information now shows the negotiated codec.
- Network drops are inferred from frame-number gaps; RTT variation is the transport's estimate, and missing measurements remain N/A.

## Steam Controller ownership

- **Exclusive Steam Controller access** in Controls → Controller Settings keeps the direct reader active while Nokcandu runs, including in the background and with mappings off. It defaults on; quit Nokcandu or switch it off to release exclusive access.
- Blocked access is shown explicitly, with **Retry Controller Access**. Closing native macOS Steam also retries paused capture. Saved profiles stay enabled through capture interruptions.
- Applies to original and 2026 Steam Controllers over their supported USB, receiver/Puck and Bluetooth paths. Other controllers remain shared. Native Steam coexistence and controller shutdown recovery still need hardware confirmation.
- Short wireless input gaps release held outputs without discarding verified capture. Missing settings replies alone no longer interrupt an otherwise live controller.

## Trackpads and the profile editor

- **Pad angle is available in every trackpad behavior**, including Scroll wheel (circle) and Existing mappings. Existing touch mappings and right-pad stick axes now honor the setting too; the 2026 physical joystick stays independent.
- The left trackpad defaults to **0°**, with straight up/down and left/right axes. Explicit saved angles are preserved. Circular scrolling keeps its step count and direction while its tap/click zones rotate.
- **A more accurate Steam Controller 2026 picture:** higher trackpads fully inside the shell, corrected stick/button positions and body proportions, inward-angled pads, QAM between the pads and gyro below the controller. Mapping labels have dedicated space and full hover text, keeping long names clear of nearby controls.
- Optional controller diagnostics show report/callback arrival rates and gaps, distinguishing raw HID reports from changed-value callbacks. These measure delivery to the app, not a certified hardware polling rate.
