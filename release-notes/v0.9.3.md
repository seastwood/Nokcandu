**My Profiles** — keep your own profiles on your Mac and share only when you choose.

- **Save Setup as Profile…** on any game saves its complete setup as a private profile (e.g. "Windowed", "High FPS"). Apply it again any time — to that game or another — with Undo.
- **Game Profiles → My Profiles**: rename, export, delete (to the Trash), and **Share with Everyone…** when you're ready. Import File adds profiles you were sent (checked first — settings only). Shared ones are marked.
- Game profiles have their own home: **[Nokcandu-Profiles](https://github.com/seastwood/Nokcandu-Profiles)** (website: https://seastwood.github.io/Nokcandu-Profiles/). Existing profiles moved with their votes and comments; Nokcandu's own Issues are for the app again. Older versions keep working through a copy of the catalog at the old address.
