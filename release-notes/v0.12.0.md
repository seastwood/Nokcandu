## New in 0.12.0

- **Wine 11 engines.** Games and launchers run on Sikarugir's Wine 11 engines, including the resizable window. Mouse and keyboard work, and Steam, EA and Battle.net show their pages and resize properly. Nokcandu checks each engine before installing its Mac driver and keeps the engine's own driver where it wouldn't fit.
- **Each game keeps its own settings inside a launcher.** Battle.net games now have their own Graphics setting. Steam and Battle.net games switch to their own graphics mode and virtual screen size as they start, so switching games no longer restarts Steam.
- **Graphics modes show their renderer and DirectX versions**: D9VK, DXVK, D3DMetal, DXMT, cnc-ddraw and WineD3D.
- **Microphone per game.** Off by default: a game without it never asks for microphone permission. Turn it on for games with voice chat.
- **Window style: Fullscreen.** Besides Borderless, games can open in Mac full screen in the resizable window.
- **Quitting is clearer.** The close button always asks before quitting a game, and quitting Steam's window quits Steam.
- **Discord** and other Sikarugir engine features are used where available; Settings → Credits lists the projects behind them.
- The resizable window setting now lives in each game's Display section.

Known: Ubisoft Connect's pages on Wine 11 haven't been checked yet. Starting a Battle.net game from Nokcandu while Battle.net is open may leave you on its page; press Play there.
