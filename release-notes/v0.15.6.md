# Nokcandu 0.15.6

## Games

- **Fixed**: after a restart, games on a games volume (the Mac-formatted volume Nokcandu keeps on an ExFAT drive) could say "Connect … to play" with the drive plugged in. macOS attached the volume, checked it (it hadn't been closed cleanly), mounted it and then unmounted it again, so it looked open while its games were unreachable. Nokcandu and its login helper now mount such a volume again, and the helper keeps checking for two minutes after a drive appears. A volume you eject stays ejected.
