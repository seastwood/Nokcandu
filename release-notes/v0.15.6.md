# Nokcandu 0.15.6

## Games

- **Fixed**: after a restart, games on a games volume (the Mac-formatted volume Nokcandu keeps on an ExFAT drive) could say "Connect … to play" with the drive plugged in. macOS attached the volume, checked it (it hadn't been closed cleanly), mounted it and then unmounted it again, so it looked open while its games were unreachable. Nokcandu and its login helper now mount such a volume again, and the helper keeps checking for two minutes after a drive appears. A volume you eject stays ejected.
- **Other login items that open a games volume are pointed out.** If another login item (a launchd agent or daemon) also opens one of your games volumes, the Games sidebar shows "Login item conflict…" and Storage → External Drives explains it, with **Turn Off**: it stops that item and renames its file to end in `.turned-off-by-nokcandu`, so renaming it back restores it. Items installed for all users are shown with what to remove, since that needs an administrator.

## Controllers

- **Testing a profile in Nokcandu no longer drives Nokcandu itself.** With a profile live and Nokcandu in front, a button mapped to a letter jumped the sidebar to the first game or profile starting with it, and Return, Space or the arrows pressed buttons or moved the selection. Keys a controller types now don't reach Nokcandu's own lists and buttons; the editor's Test area still shows everything the game gets. Typing into a text field with a controller and the Controller Input Test window work as before. Mouse movement and clicks from the controller are unchanged.

- **Trackpad touch settings** (Steam Controller trackpads, in each pad's settings under Touch). Both are off by default, so every touch counts as before:
  - **Ignore brief brushes**: a touch shorter than the Shortest tap (10–150 ms) isn't a tap, so grazing a zone does nothing.
  - **Need a firm press** (trackpads that report pressure: the 2026 Steam Controller): taps, zones and held touches count only once your thumb presses at least the Pressure needed. Moving the pointer and clicking aren't affected. Not yet tried on the controller itself.
  - To act only on a full press on any Steam Controller, set a zone's Click instead of its Tap.
- **Pulse while on the edge** (Steam Controller trackpads with an edge set): haptic pulses in that pad for as long as your finger is on the edge, with their strength and how often (40–600 ms) adjustable. The first comes as you reach the edge, and the rhythm holds even with your thumb still. Off by default.

## Look

- The README screenshots fill their cells: the empty margin around each window is gone. New Games and Streams pictures.
- **Fixed** (before release): the sidebar's login-item warning was long enough to push the sidebar's tabs, search field and buttons out of the window.

## Reliability

- Every test build now lays out the main window at its smallest size in each tab, with worst-case content (dozens of games with very long names, warnings showing, a game open), and stops before installing if anything doesn't fit. It runs without starting services or touching controllers or settings.
