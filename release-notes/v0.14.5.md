# Nokcandu 0.14.5

## Choose the app you need

- **Show only Games, Remapping and/or Streaming.** Choose on the welcome screen or change your selection under Settings → General → Features. Existing users keep all features visible until they choose otherwise.
- Hiding a feature preserves installed games, saves, profiles, settings and paired PCs. Turn it back on to see your content again. Active games and streams keep their session controls.
- Remapping and streaming users can skip Windows game setup. Hiding Games also suppresses Wine and game-component update notifications; Nokcandu's own updates remain available.

## Quieter, clearer updates

- The settings gear shows **red for Nokcandu updates** and **orange for game and component updates**. An app update takes priority when both are available.
- **Ignore This Version** clears an update notification without installing anything or pinning a game's Wine engine. A newer version can notify again. Restore ignored notifications from Updates at any time.
- App-update availability is retained independently of the Wine catalog, including when Games is hidden.

## Use Everywhere in Streams

- Each stream's Controller settings now offers **Let Use Everywhere override**. When enabled, the active Everywhere profile takes precedence over that stream's selected profile.
- Stopping Everywhere returns to the stream's saved selection. Existing streams keep their previous behavior until this option is enabled.

## New controller options

These additions are opt-in and have automated coverage, but physical-controller gameplay and emulator acceptance are still pending. Treat these new modes as experimental and report any problems. Existing pad modes, standard triggers and radial menus retain their behavior.

- **Absolute mouse regions:** map either Steam Controller pad directly to part of a window or screen, useful for menus and inventories. Streams use the streamed display. Pointer-locked aiming still needs relative mouse mode.
- **Drawn gestures:** record or type up to four directional gestures per Steam Controller pad and bind them to existing actions, macros or layers. Commands fire when you lift your finger.
- **Trigger modes:** fast full pulls that skip soft pull, exclusive soft/full actions, and hair-trigger release/repress behavior, with adjustable timing or travel.
- **More on-screen menus:** grid, horizontal and button-shortcut layouts alongside radial menus. Button-shortcut menus stay open until you choose an item; Steam/Guide cancels.
- **Local DSU/CemuHook motion:** optionally send raw gyro and acceleration to compatible emulators on this Mac. Configure it under Controls → Controller Settings. Default address `127.0.0.1:26760`; up to four motion sources. Buttons use the emulator's normal controller setup. Emulator gyro orientation still needs hardware validation.

The absolute-pad and gesture modes use Nokcandu's direct Steam Controller reader while the app is running. Original and 2026 controller paths are implemented; physical acceptance of these additions remains pending.
