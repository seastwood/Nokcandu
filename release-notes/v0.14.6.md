# Nokcandu 0.14.6

## Unplugging controllers

- **Fixed held buttons and sticks staying pressed after a controller was unplugged.** They stayed held until Nokcandu was restarted. Unplugging now releases everything the controller was holding, for every controller Nokcandu reads directly.
- **Fixed a crash when several devices were unplugged at once**, such as from a USB hub with a Steam Controller receiver on it.

## Games on an external drive

- **Games that run inside Steam, Battle.net, EA or Ubisoft now show "Connect <drive> to play"** when that launcher is on a drive that isn't connected. Before, they looked ready to play.
- **Games on an unplugged drive stay where they are in the sidebar**, in their own section and your own order, instead of moving to a "Drive not connected" list at the bottom. Steam and Battle.net stay under Launchers.
- Their icons stay too: Nokcandu keeps a small copy of each game's icon and shows it greyed while the drive is unplugged. A game gets its copy the next time its drive is connected.
- **Play is disabled** for a game whose drive isn't connected, with the drive's name beside it.

## Trackpads

- **New: Mouse-like stick.** Either Steam Controller trackpad can drive its side's stick like a mouse: the faster your finger moves, the further the stick tilts, and it returns to centre when your finger stops. Where you touch doesn't matter. Set how fast a swipe reaches full tilt, and optionally keep a flick going after you lift (inertia), easing off over the momentum half-life. Works in Windows games, streams and Mac games; the joystick (and the 2026 controller's right stick) still wins when pushed further. Experimental: tell us how it feels.
- **Absolute mouse regions can be larger than 100%** (up to 200%), so the original Steam Controller's round pads can reach the corners of the screen. Around 140% reaches them. Settings up to 100% are unchanged.
