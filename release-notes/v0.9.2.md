**Game Profiles, organized and complete**

- Profiles are grouped by game: one row per game with its number of profiles, total 👍, best rating and last update. Games with the most 👍 come first; sort by recently updated or A–Z, filter by Steam / Battle.net / other. Within a game, the most-liked profile is on top and ones reported broken are tucked away. The website works the same way.
- Every profile shows when it was shared and last updated, the Mac, macOS, engine and Nokcandu version it was tested with, and flags ones tested on a different engine.
- Profiles now carry the complete setup: the game's program, graphics, launch options, every extra setting (paths inside the game's app are shared portably), raw mouse, performance, the cinematic fix, drive letters, disc image and Battle.net's own launch options. Applying a profile makes the game match it exactly — e.g. a profile with no launch options removes yours — and Undo puts yours back.
- Share This Setup starts from your "Works"/"Info" notes and lists anything that can't be shared and why (only settings that would load files from outside the game's app).
- Apple Metal now sets `CX_APPLEGPTK_LIBD3DSHARED_PATH` automatically for each game.
