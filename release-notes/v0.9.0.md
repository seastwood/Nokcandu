First public release.

**Requirements:** Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14 or newer. Nokcandu installs Rosetta 2 and downloads its tested Wine engine on first launch (about 260 MB).

**First open:** Nokcandu isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. If macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Only needed once; Nokcandu updates itself after that.

Highlights:
- Turn the Windows games you own into Mac apps: Windows installers, disc images, existing folders, or Steam.
- One shared Steam and one shared Battle.net for all your games, each game with its own Mac app and icon.
- Raw mouse for aiming in shooters, performance switches (FPS counter, vsync, frame limit), drive letters for external SSDs, storage cleanup.
- Fix cinematic playback, Repair, Diagnostics, per-game notes.
- Keeps itself and its Wine engine up to date, with self-tests and one-click roll back.
