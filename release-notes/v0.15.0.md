# Nokcandu 0.15.0

## More controllers
Nokcandu now reads many controllers macOS doesn't know, with drivers of its own. Each works in controller profiles like any other controller, and is its own player in a stream. Most of these were written from the controllers' documented protocols without the hardware at hand: if one misbehaves, `~/Library/Logs/Nokcandu/Controllers.log` says how it was read, and a report is very welcome.

- **Steam Controller (2026)**: by USB, its Puck and Bluetooth, with its own picture in the profile editor. Both sticks, trackpads with pressure, gyro, haptics and rumble; stick touch and Grip Sense are inputs you can map. Trackpads have no smoothing by default.
- **Generic and third-party gamepads**: controllers macOS doesn't recognise are read as standard controllers using the community controller database, or a best-guess layout for a gamepad that isn't in it.
- **PlayStation 3 controller** by USB.
- **Switch 2 Pro Controller, GameCube controller (Switch 2) and Joy-Con 2** by USB, with gyro and rumble. A left and a right Joy-Con 2 are one controller.
- **Xbox 360 wired, Xbox One and Series wired** (when macOS doesn't read them) and compatible controllers, with rumble. The **Xbox 360 Wireless Receiver** gives up to four controllers, each with its light and rumble.
- **GameCube controller adapter** (Wii U / Switch): four ports, four players, rumble with its power plug connected.
- **Wii Remote** (alone, with a Nunchuk or a Classic Controller) and the **Wii U Pro Controller** over Bluetooth, with the remote's accelerometer and a MotionPlus gyro.
- **8BitDo** SF30/SN30 Pro, Pro 2, Pro 3 and Ultimate 2/2C/3 in D-input mode: back buttons, Share, gyro and rumble.
- **Google Stadia**, **Amazon Luna**, **NVIDIA SHIELD**, **ZUIKI** EVOTOP/AXIS/MASCON PRO, **HORI** Wireless HORIPAD for Steam, **GameSir** G7 Pro 8K and Tarantula 8K, and **SInput** controllers (GP2040-CE, ProGCC, Firebird…): their extra buttons, and gyro and rumble where they have them.
- **PlayStation 4 and 5 compatible controllers** macOS doesn't read (HORI, Nacon, Razer, PDP and others): gyro, touchpad click and, by USB, rumble.
- **DualSense Edge** paddles and the Capture/Share buttons are named in the profile editor.

A controller macOS already reads, or one a profile reads as its own device, is left exactly as it was.

## Controller profiles
- **Export All Controller Profiles…** (in New Controller Profile, or right-click a profile) saves every profile in one zip, a file per profile: a backup, a way to move to another Mac, or something to share.
- **Drop to import.** Drop that zip on Nokcandu, or any single profile file, or several, or a folder of them. Import from File… takes the same things.
- Importing never overwrites a profile. One that is already here with the same name and settings is left alone, so a backup can be imported again without doubling everything; one that differs comes in beside yours as a copy.
- **Save as File…** is also in a profile's right-click menu.

## Rumble and motion
- Each controller Nokcandu reads itself has its own rumble and gyro. At the desktop a profile's rumble goes to the controllers that feed it.
- Commands to a controller are sent off the main thread, so a slow Bluetooth controller can't hold the app up.

## Streams
- **Controllers Nokcandu reads itself are players too**, each with the host's rumble for that player and its own gyro.
- **Each controller is its own player** now also works with a controller profile: the first controller goes through the profile as player one, and every other controller is sent to the host as itself.
